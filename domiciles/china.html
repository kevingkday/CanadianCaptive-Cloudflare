China

China licenses captive insurers, called captive insurance companies in the National Financial Regulatory Administration's licensing guide, under item 409 of the Decision of the State Council on Setting Administrative Licensing for Administrative Examination and Approval Items That Need to Be Retained, State Council Order No. 412. That decision took effect on 1 July 2004 and assigns approval of the establishment, merger, division, change and dissolution of an exclusive captive organisation. The Administration's 2023 licensing guide, and its 2025 guide, still list the Insurance Law, the Provisions on the Administration of Insurance Companies, the Notice on Matters Concerning the Supervision of Captive Insurance Companies, and the Notice on Further Improving Matters Concerning the Supervision of Captive Insurance Companies as conditions for captive preparation and opening. The full texts of those two notices were not available as live Administration or State Council pages, so their parent-company asset threshold, business-line list and any captive-specific capital formula are not restated. Where the Insurance Law applies, Article 69 requires registered capital of at least RMB 200 million, paid in cash. The supervisor may set a higher floor and may not set a lower one. The Administration's legal-person list as at 31 December 2025 identifies four property insurers whose Chinese or English names identify them as captives: CNPC Captive Insurance, China Railway Captive Insurance, COSCO Shipping Captive Insurance, and Guangdong Energy Captive Insurance. The list does not use a separate captive institution type, and it does not publish formations or surrenders. No captive-specific tax exemption was stated in the Insurance Law text or the licensing guide. Enterprise income tax is 25 percent. Value-added tax on insurance services, as a financial service, is 6 percent for a general taxpayer.

Regulatory Framework

Decision of the State Council on Setting Administrative Licensing for Administrative Examination and Approval Items That Need to Be Retained, State Council Order No. 412, item 409, and the Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China

Applicable Statutes & Regulations

State Council Order No. 412 was promulgated on 29 June 2004 and took effect on 1 July 2004. Item 409 retains licensing for the establishment, merger, division, change and dissolution of an exclusive captive organisation and a mutual insurance organisation. The decision states that procedures and time limits follow the Administrative License Law. The National Financial Regulatory Administration's licensing guide of 16 June 2023 lists, as conditions for captive preparation and opening, the Insurance Law, the Provisions on the Administration of Insurance Companies, the Notice on Matters Concerning the Supervision of Captive Insurance Companies, the Notice on Further Improving Matters Concerning the Supervision of Captive Insurance Companies, and several general insurer rules on shareholders, plans, articles and senior officers. The Administration's 2025 licensing guide still cites Order No. 412, item 409, as the approval basis and still cites the first captive notice for a change of name. The full texts and document numbers of the two captive notices were not on the Administration or State Council pages retrieved. The Administration's cleanup of 24 December 2025 did not list either notice among the normative documents it repealed or declared spent. The Insurance Law text published by the Administration sets capital in Article 69, preparation and opening periods in Articles 71 and 73, reporting in Article 86, and solvency supervision in Article 137. The 2019 related-party rules state that a captive's captive business is outside those rules. The 2019 corporate-governance assessment includes captive companies and says that separate rules on captive shareholders and governance prevail where they exist.

Licensing Speed
State Council Order No. 412 states that procedures and time limits for the retained licensing items follow the Administrative License Law. Insurance Law Article 71 requires a decision on preparation of an insurance company within six months after acceptance. Article 73 requires a decision on opening within 60 days after acceptance of the opening application. The captive items in the Administration's 2023 licensing guide cite Order No. 412 and do not restate those Insurance Law periods as the captive timetable.
Premium Tax Rate
Enterprise income tax of 25 percent under Article 4 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law. Value-added tax of 6 percent on insurance services for a general taxpayer. No separate premium-tax rate, and no captive-specific exemption from either tax, was stated in the Insurance Law text or the licensing guide.
Incorporation / Initial Fee
No captive application fee or licence-issue fee was stated in the Administration's captive licensing guide or in the Insurance Law articles reviewed.
Annual Maintenance Fee
No captive annual supervisory fee was stated in the Administration's captive licensing guide. The Administration's cleanup of 24 December 2025 declared spent the former notice that had temporarily suspended insurance regulatory fees. That cleanup does not publish a replacement fee amount.
Annual Filing Requirements
Article 86 of the Insurance Law requires an insurance company to submit the reports, statements, documents and materials prescribed by the insurance supervisor. Solvency reports, financial accounting reports, actuarial reports, compliance reports and the other prescribed materials must record the insurance business truthfully. Article 137 requires the supervisor to establish a solvency supervision system and to monitor the solvency of insurance companies. The Administration's licensing guide lists the Insurance Law among the conditions for a captive and does not restate a captive-only filing calendar. Any quarterly captive report in the captive notices is not restated, because those notices were not retrieved from a live Administration or State Council page. The 2019 related-party measures state that they do not apply to a captive's captive business. The institution page publishes a petition-consultation telephone, 010-66279113, on working-day mornings and afternoons, and the banking and insurance consumer-complaint line 12378. It does not publish a general email address.
